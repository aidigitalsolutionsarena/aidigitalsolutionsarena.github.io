The Challenge

The hardest part is often knowing what to say next.

Real estate conversations can move quickly. Agents may need to generate leads, respond to enquiries, follow up, answer objections and keep prospects engaged — often while managing several prospects at the same time.

  • Not knowing how to start a conversation with a prospect
  • Struggling to keep a buyer engaged
  • Running out of ideas for follow-up messages
  • Finding it difficult to respond to objections
  • Needing practical wording for different sales situations

A prompt gives you a starting point.

Instead of staring at a blank chat box and wondering what to ask, use structured prompts to help guide your AI tool toward the type of response, message, idea or sales support you need.

You can then review, personalize and adapt the output to fit your actual prospect, property and professional style.
